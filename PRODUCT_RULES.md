# PRODUCT_RULES

## Order Confirmation And Payment Principles

1. Users finish item selection on the menu page and only confirm the order on the confirmation sheet.
2. The order confirmation sheet is not a second menu page.
3. Selected items stay collapsed by default; users expand them only when they need to review or edit.
4. Table number, item count, discount, balance deduction, and payable amount must be visible at a glance.
5. Users confirm an order only once.
6. The WeChat payment system screen is the final payment confirmation.
7. Do not show another in-app business confirmation or custom payment confirmation after the pay button.
8. Marketing and membership content must not compete with order confirmation.
9. Backend calculation is authoritative for final payable amounts; frontend amounts are display estimates.
10. Removing one meaningless confirmation step is preferred to adding another explanation or feature.
